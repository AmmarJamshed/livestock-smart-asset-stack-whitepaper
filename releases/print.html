Executive summary

Why this is novel

Apps, traceability pilots, exchanges, and virtual-asset talk already exist. Novelty = wiring them into one farm-to-market process.

What success looks like

  • Verified animal promoted into a registry title
  • Title carries a vet-desk health grade on a defined clock
  • Only passing stock underpins a PVARA-compliant token
  • A daily value board publishes prices with verified-inventory %
  • Banks/insurers price credit and cover off the stack — not notebooks

The problem

The problem

Broken fence: animal wealth is huge on the farm — but off-book for banks, insurers, and markets.

Three failures that keep animal wealth “off-book”

1. The ear tag washes away

Paper tags, notebooks, and village memory collapse under floods, theft disputes, and cross-district sales. When water rises, proof of ownership disappears with the paper. Biometric and national-traceability tools exist because this pain is real — but they are not yet wired into capital markets.

2. The vet note never leaves the yard

A buyer, lender, or insurer rarely gets a standardized, signed health feed tied to a durable animal ID. Outbreak risk stays informal. Without portable health grades, insurance pricing and token mint gates cannot be honest.

3. The mandi board never becomes a national board

Live animal and poultry value is discovered in mandis and farm-gate deals that do not produce a national daily value surface. Capital markets see equities and bonds — not a clean livestock grid. Illiquidity is structural, not accidental.

Why “just build a farm app” is not enough

An app can register a cow. A smart asset stack must also answer title, access, attestation, mint, and price questions.

Question the stack must answer Farm metaphor Layer
Who is the legal issuer of record? Title desk Registry company
Who may query (bank, insurer, exchange)? Who holds the keys Access control
What upgrades a barn note into a market health feed? Vet desk stamp Health feeds
When may a token be minted or frozen? Green lane / red pen PVARA mint gate
How does a price get published safely? Market board Value grid

Design principle

Identity without custody is a database.
Custody without identity is fiction.
Price without both is noise.

SLAS requires identity + health + token + market rails before claiming livestock is “on the grid.”

Registry company

Registry company

L1 — Legal spine. Your notes correctly struck “Access” and circled company. A query portal is not enough.

Notebook origin

Pakistan needs a registry company — a legal person that issues and maintains animal / batch identities, governs who may read or write them, and can interface with custody (CDC sub) and health rails.

Job of the registry

  1. Accept promoted field IDs (Animal Passport / PAITS / peers)
  2. Issue a canonical registry key (system of record)
  3. Bind owner / aggregator KYC to that key
  4. Expose controlled APIs to banks, insurers, token issuers, and (later) market data agents
  5. Maintain dispute, re-tag, death, and transfer events
sequenceDiagram
  participant Farm as Farmer / aggregator
  participant AP as Animal Passport / PAITS
  participant REG as Registry company
  participant BANK as Bank / insurer
  participant CDC as CDC custody sub
  Farm->>AP: Register / verify animal
  AP->>REG: Promote ID + owner binding
  REG->>REG: Issue canonical key + access ACL
  BANK->>REG: Query eligibility / ownership
  REG-->>BANK: Attested record (scoped)
  REG->>CDC: Optional book-entry link for entitlements

Design choices to settle in the paper’s pilot

Choice Options Lean for MVP
Legal form Section company / utility / concession Narrow-purpose company with clear public-interest charter
ID source of truth Field app vs registry Registry wins; field apps are edges
Access model Open vs licensed queriers Licensed queriers (banks, insurers, issuers)
CDC link Subsidiary vs special accounts Phased: accounts first, sub later if volume justifies
Poultry vs cattle Per-animal vs batch IDs Cattle: per-head; poultry: batch / flock IDs

Must-have data fields (minimum)

  • Canonical registry ID
  • Field keys (passport biometric ref, PAITS ID, ear tag)
  • Species / breed / sex / approx. age or DOB estimate
  • Owner / aggregator identifiers (CNIC / NTN as applicable)
  • Status: active · transferred · dead · quarantined · disputed
  • Last health grade pointer (to L2 feed)
  • Audit log hash / version

Failure modes

  • Double registration across apps → merge rules and biometric match thresholds
  • Owner disputes → escrow + local verification workflow
  • Ghost animals for credit → mandatory field re-verify before token mint or large loans

Health assessment & daily feeds

L2 — Farm health rail (amber / hay). Barn notes and AI scores are the raw feed. Markets need a signed daily health grade — like an ear-tag that banks and token gates can read.

Role in the stack

Farm side (inputs) Market side (outputs)
Passport / farm-app health events Attested daily health feed
Local vet & lab notes Grade A / B / C / Quarantine

Proposed institutional path from the notebook: clinical credibility (e.g. AKUH standards path) plus public-health policy (Ministry of Health) with livestock / veterinary ministries. These are design counterparts, not claimed partners.

PVARA tokenization

L3 — Sky layer. Tokens mint only after L1 identity + L2 health pass. Notebook: PVARA → Livestock Tokenization & Poultry.

What the token can represent (pick explicitly)

Object Fits Caution
Single large animal economic claim Breeding / fattening cattle Welfare & delivery logistics
Flock / batch claim Poultry cycles Batch ID discipline
Pooled herd share Diversified risk NAV methodology
Cashflow claim (milk / eggs) Shorter tenor products Oracle dependence

Mint gate (non-negotiable)

MAY_MINT =
  registry.status == active
  AND health.grade in {A, B}
  AND health.validity_until > now
  AND no open dispute
  AND not already pledged beyond limit

Custody path

  • Phase 1: issuer register + off-exchange transfer rules
  • Phase 2: CDC book-entry entitlements (notebook: CDC sub)
  • Phase 3: optional PSX-visible products once liquidity and disclosure mature

Compliance posture (independent framing)

This paper does not assert a final legal classification. The design assumes issuers will seek clarity under Pakistan’s evolving virtual-asset / securities perimeter (PVARA and related authorities). Shariah review should be treated as a first-class workstream for farmer and retail acceptance.

Anti-abuse

  • Biometric / PAITS re-verify before large mints
  • One registry ID → controlled pledge graph (no silent double-count)
  • Automatic freeze hooks from L2 quarantine flags

How to achieve it: roadmap

How to achieve it: roadmap

Do not flip every switch on day one. Pakistan should execute this as a phased national rollout — one province, one species, one layer at a time — then expand only when exit criteria pass.

This is a build sequence for practitioners and policymakers — not an institutional mandate. The stack is designed for pilot → prove → scale, never “big bang.”

Golden rule for Pakistan

Wrong approach Right approach
Launch tokens + PSX listing + national registry together Start with identity + registry in one district
Mandate every farmer overnight Onboard through aggregators / progressive farms first
Treat PSX/CDC as week-one requirements Keep them for later phases after feeds are honest
Copy a foreign “full stack” and hope Use local rails (PAITS, Passport-class apps, PVARA perimeter) in order

Purpose: agree what “done” means before writing code.

Outcomes

  • Freeze SLAS vocabulary (this whitepaper)
  • Choose one province + one species (e.g. Sindh cattle or Punjab broilers — not both)
  • Choose integration model (B — dual-rail recommended)
  • Draft one-pagers for registry counsel, one edge platform, one bank, one insurer

Exit criteria: non-binding interest letters from ≥3 parties.

If Phase 0 cannot name a single pilot geography, stop. National ambition without a yard to test in becomes theatre.

Purpose: prove animals can be promoted into a legal registry — nothing else.

  • Promote API from Animal Passport-class edge (+ PAITS mapping where available)
  • Owner KYC lite + dispute workflow
  • Bank read-only eligibility API

Exit criteria: 1,000+ promoted live IDs (or equivalent poultry batches) with <2% unresolved disputes.

Still off-limits in Phase 1: retail tokens, PSX listings, national mandates.

Phase 2 — Health feeds (8–14 weeks, after identity works)

Purpose: attach portable grades to registry IDs.

Build

  • Publish SLAS-HEALTH-0.1
  • Onboard attestors (vets / labs)
  • Daily grade jobs + quarantine freeze hooks

Exit criteria: ≥70% of pilot inventory has a non-expired grade.

Phase 3 — Controlled tokenization sandbox (10–16 weeks)

Purpose: test mint gates on sandbox assets — not a public market launch.

Build

  • Legal memo under PVARA perimeter
  • Mint gate (identity + health + pledge graph)
  • Disclosures + Shariah review track
  • Optional internal transfer register

Exit criteria: clean test mint/burn with zero double-count incidents.

Phase 3 is a sandbox. Public retail products wait until phases 1–2 are boringly reliable.

Phase 4 — Value grid & market visibility (12–20 weeks)

Purpose: publish honest daily prices for the pilot region; talk to PSX as an information product first.

Build

  • Private daily value feed + pct_verified_inventory
  • Bank collateral simulation
  • PSX Stage-1 information-product conversations (not rushed listing)
  • CDC custody design workshop (still phased)

Exit criteria: 60 consecutive publish days; ≥1 financial institution consumes the feed in a pilot credit/insurance process.

Phase 5 — Scale across provinces (only after proof)

Purpose: copy what worked — do not redesign under pressure.

Rules for scale

  1. Second province only after Phase 4 exit criteria hold for one full quarter
  2. Add poultry or cattle second — not both in the same wave
  3. Keep vendor interchangeability (certify edge apps; don’t lock the nation to one)
  4. Expand PSX/CDC roles only when verified inventory % stays high

Why phases protect Pakistan

Risk of “all at once” What phasing prevents
Fake inventory minted as tokens Mint only after identity + health gates work
Farmer distrust Start voluntary / aggregator-led, prove benefit
Exchange brand damage PSX sees clean feeds before any listing talk
Policy whiplash Each phase has exit criteria before the next unlocks

Why this topic

Livestock and poultry are central to Pakistani livelihoods, yet remain hard to finance cleanly when identity, health, and price discovery do not travel together. Platforms such as Animal Passport show that field identity can work; capital-market rails show that custody and price can work. The missing piece is a smart stack that connects them without pretending any single institution already owns the whole design. This paper is an attempt to put that stack on one page — independently.
