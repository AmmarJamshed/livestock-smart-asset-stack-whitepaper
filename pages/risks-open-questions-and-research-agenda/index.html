Risks, open questions & research agenda

Material risks

Risk Why it matters Mitigation
Vendor capture One app becomes de facto registry Registry as system of record; certify multiple edges
Fake inventory Tokens on ghost animals Biometric re-verify; pledge graph; attestor liability
Health theater Grades without audits Protocol + surprise audits + freeze hooks
Premature listing PSX brand used before methodology mature Stage-1 information products first
Regulatory fog Token class unclear Sandbox + counsel; no retail until clarity
Farmer exclusion Digitization without access Free/low-cost capture; aggregator model; Urdu UX
Zoonosis politics MoH vs livestock turf Explicit dual-ministry RACI

Open research questions

  1. What biometric FAR/FRR is acceptable for credit-scale mint gates in Pakistani field conditions (wet muzzle, dust, low light)?
  2. How should poultry batch IDs inherit and shed birds without breaking token accounting?
  3. Can PAITS keys and private biometric IDs be linked with privacy-preserving joins?
  4. What is the minimum disclosure pack for a PVARA-perimeter livestock token?
  5. Which PSX product type (data, index, listed) maximizes farmer benefit vs investor confusion?
  6. How do Islamic finance structures map onto fattening cycles and flock turns?

Honest limit of v1

This document is a design whitepaper, not an empirical evaluation and not a prospectus. Its job is to make the smart process legible enough that practitioners can agree — or disagree — in public.
