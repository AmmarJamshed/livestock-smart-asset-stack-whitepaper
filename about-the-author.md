### Why this topic

Livestock and poultry are central to Pakistani livelihoods, yet remain hard to finance cleanly when identity, health, and price discovery do not travel together. Platforms such as Animal Passport show that **field identity** can work; capital-market rails show that **custody and price** can work. The missing piece is a **smart stack** that connects them without pretending any single institution already owns the whole design. This paper is an attempt to put that stack on one page — independently.
